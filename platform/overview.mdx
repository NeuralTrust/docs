Every NeuralTrust tenant is called a **Team**. A team is the top-level container that holds your users, roles, integrations, audit history, and the products provisioned for you (TrustGate, TrustLens, TrustTest, and anything else). **Team Settings** is the admin surface where you configure the team itself — everything that lives *above* the individual products.

Open it from the left-hand nav → **Team settings**. The docs mirror the sidebar and are organized into the same themed groups.

## Who can do what

Access to Team Settings is gated by **team role** — an organization-wide role that sits above per-product roles.

| Role | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control of the team. Can change the team name, delete the team, configure SSO, manage break-glass users, rotate SCIM tokens, provision data planes, and toggle feature flags. |
| **Admin** | Day-to-day administration. Manages users and invitations, verifies email domains, edits group mappings, reviews audit logs, and configures SIEM and password policy. |
| **Member** | Uses the products they have access to. Cannot see Team Settings. |

Individual products (TrustGate, TrustTest, …) have their own role model on top of this — a team Admin is not automatically a product admin, and vice versa.

## Recommended setup order

For a fresh team, configure Team Settings in this order:

1. **[General](/platform/general)** — set a human-readable team name.
2. **[SSO Configuration](/platform/sso)** — pick Microsoft Entra ID or [generic OIDC](/platform/generic-oidc-sso).
3. **[Break-glass access](/platform/break-glass)** — add at least one emergency user *before* enforcing SSO.
4. **[Password Policy](/platform/password-policy)** — tighten local passwords for accounts that keep them.
5. **[SCIM Provisioning](/platform/scim)** *(Entra ID)* — automate user creation and removal.
6. **[Group mappings](/platform/user-sync)** — map IdP groups to NeuralTrust roles.
7. **[Users](/platform/users)** — invite anyone not provisioned by SCIM and assign per-product access.
8. **[Integrations](/platform/alert-integrations)** — forward alert findings to your SIEM.
9. **[Custom Domain](/platform/custom-domain)** — if you want the app on your own hostname.
10. **[Advanced](/platform/advanced)** — provision a hybrid data plane if you're on a Hybrid plan.

## Prerequisites

- **NeuralTrust account** with **Owner** or **Admin** role.
- For SSO: administrator access to your identity provider.
- For hybrid provisioning: admin access to the target AWS / GCP / Azure account.
- For custom domain: control over the DNS zone of the domain you want to use.
