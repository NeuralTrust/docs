<p className="nt-lead">
  TrustForce reads email to protect people, not to keep it. It stores the
  least it needs to show evidence and results, and deletes it on a schedule
  you control.
</p>

## What it stores, and what it doesn't

| Data | Stored | Never stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reported and flagged emails** | Sender, subject, the first few hundred characters of text, selected headers, links, authentication results, detection results, the AI assessment, attachment names and hashes | The full body and the attachments |
| **Email that looks fine** | Daily counters, plus a 30-day index of message IDs, link domains and attachment hashes, so a later decision reaches every copy | The content |
| **Sender history** | External addresses your employees exchange mail with, read from received mail and **Sent Items**, with message counts. It tells a known sender from a first-time one | The content |
| **Simulations** | Who received which simulation, and when they opened, clicked, submitted or reported it. For AI voice calls, a redacted transcript of what the person said | Anything typed into a landing page, and the IP address or device of whoever clicked |
| **Employees** | Name, email, department, groups and, depending on your source, title, manager, phone, start date, location, country and language | |

**View original** reads a message live from the mailbox when an Admin needs
it, and stores nothing. It requires a reason and is recorded in the
[platform logs](/platform/audit-logs).

### AI and outside lookups

- For mail that rules alone can't settle, an AI model reads a **redacted
  excerpt** of the message. The excerpt is not stored.
- **VirusTotal** receives file hashes and domains only. Nothing is uploaded.
- Roles and group tiers for the [risk score](/employee-security/employees/risk-score#impact-role-and-access)
  are estimated from job titles and group names, never from names or email
  addresses.

## Retention

Set in **Settings › Employee Security › Data retention**. A daily job deletes
what's past its period.

| Setting | Default | Range | What is deleted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tracking events (days)** | 90 | 30–730 | The individual open, click and submit events of simulations |
| **Email content (days)** | 180 | 7–365 | The content kept for reported emails. The verdict stays |
| **Inbound detections (days)** | Same as email content | 7–3650 | Who sent what in flagged emails. The verdict and the action taken stay, and message IDs too, so a quarantined copy can still be restored |

Each person's outcome in a campaign, and the transcripts of AI voice calls,
stay with the campaign's results. Sender history is deleted after 13 months
without activity.

## Offboarding and deletion

Offboarding someone keeps their history, so campaign and training results
don't change after people leave. To delete a person's data entirely, contact
NeuralTrust support.

## Protection

- **Isolation:** each organization's data is separated at the database level
  with row-level security.
- **Secrets:** integration credentials, such as the Microsoft 365 client
  secret, are encrypted at rest. API keys are stored hashed and shown only
  once.
- **No Google key to leak:** Google Workspace uses domain-wide delegation to
  NeuralTrust's client ID. You never create or upload a service account key,
  and access is limited to your verified domains.
- **Least privilege in the mail platform:** one Microsoft Graph permission,
  two Gmail scopes, and no directory access. See
  [Microsoft 365](/employee-security/connect/microsoft-365) and
  [Google Workspace](/employee-security/connect/google-workspace).

For NeuralTrust's platform-wide practices, see [Security](/neuraltrust/security/overview)
and [Data privacy](/neuraltrust/data-privacy/overview).
