Employees report suspicious email with the buttons their mail app already has. There is no add-in to install.

- Reports of real emails appear in [**Email security › Emails**](/employee-security/email-security/triage), credited to the employee who reported them. Once your team decides, the employee gets an email with the outcome.
- Reports of NeuralTrust phishing simulations count as **reported**, and the employee gets an email confirming it was a simulation.

---

## Google Workspace

Nothing to set up. Employees use Gmail's **Report phishing** or **Report spam**, on the web or in the Gmail app.

<Note>
Gmail doesn't say whether the employee chose phishing or spam: Email security reads the email and gives its own verdict. An email reported and then deleted for good before it's read is missed.
</Note>

## Microsoft 365

Employees use Outlook's built-in **Report** button. Microsoft sends each report to a reporting mailbox, and NeuralTrust reads it from there with the Microsoft 365 connection you already set up.

It works in Outlook on the web, the new Outlook for Windows, Outlook for Microsoft 365, Outlook for Mac, and Outlook for iOS and Android, in recent versions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a reporting mailbox">
    Create a dedicated Exchange Online mailbox for reports, for example `phishing-reports@yourdomain.com`, on one of your verified domains. Set it to accept mail from internal senders only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Outlook's reports to it">
    In the [Microsoft Defender portal](https://security.microsoft.com/securitysettings/userSubmission), open **Settings › Email & collaboration › User reported settings** and:

    1. Check **Monitor reported messages in Outlook**.
    2. Select **Use the built-in Report button in Outlook**.
    3. Under **Send reported items to**, choose **My reporting mailbox only** (or **Microsoft and my reporting mailbox**) and add the mailbox.
    4. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver reports unfiltered">
    In [Advanced delivery](https://security.microsoft.com/advanceddelivery), add the mailbox as a **SecOps mailbox**. If you use data loss prevention, exclude the mailbox from it too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it in NeuralTrust">
    In the NeuralTrust console, open **Email security › Settings › Report with the built-in buttons**, enter the mailbox and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test report">
    Report any email with Outlook's **Report** button. **Last report received** appears next to the mailbox in the console.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you deployed the NeuralTrust Outlook add-in earlier, remove it in the Microsoft 365 admin center under **Settings › Integrated apps**.

### How reports are handled

- A report is accepted only when it comes from inside your organization and from an active employee.
- **Junk** and **Phishing** reports open a report in Email security. **Not junk** reports are ignored.
- The reporting mailbox is never scanned, quarantined or tagged. Its messages are left as they are.
- An employee reporting from a shared mailbox needs **Send As** permission on it; without it, Microsoft removes the message but doesn't send the report.
