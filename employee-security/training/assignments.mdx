<p className="nt-lead">
  An assignment says who takes which courses and by when. Set up the annual
  awareness training once, and let onboarding and refreshers run by
  themselves.
</p>

**Training › Assignments** lists every assignment with its type, status,
modules, enrolled people, completion, overdue people and due date. Click **New
assignment** to create one; you need the **Editor** role for Employee
Security.

## Types

| Type | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **One-Time** | A course everyone in the audience takes once, by a due date |
| **Recurring** | Training that repeats: **Every 3 months**, **Every 6 months** or **Every year** |
| **Trigger-based** | Enrolls people when they **Join the organization**, **Are added to the selected user groups**, or **Fail 2+ phishing simulations in 90 days** |

Campaigns can also assign a lesson right after someone fails a simulation. See
[Campaigns](/employee-security/phishing/campaigns#create-a-campaign).

## Create an assignment

The wizard has five steps: **Details**, **Audience** (all employees, user
groups or individuals), **Content** (courses or a collection), **Schedule**
and **Review**. **Due within** sets how many days people have to finish: 7,
14 (the default), 30 or 60.

## Invitations and reminders

- **Invitations** go out by email when the assignment starts: right away, or
  on the start date you pick. One email lists every course in the assignment.
- **Reminders** go out automatically 3 days before the due date and on the
  due date, at most one per person and assignment per day.
- **Send reminder** in the assignment sends one now, optionally **Only** to
  the overdue employees.

Invitations and reminders are placed in the mailbox through your
[mail connection](/employee-security/overview#how-it-reaches-your-employees),
in the employee's language (English, Spanish, German or French).

## Follow progress

The assignment's panel shows a timeline (created, invitations sent, reminders,
due date, new rounds) and a **People** tab with each person's status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not started** | Invited, hasn't opened it |
| **In progress** | Started, not passed yet |
| **Complete** | Passed |
| **Overdue** | Past the due date without passing |

**Training › Overview** sums it up: active assignments, enrolled employees,
completion rate, overdue people, and completion by user group.

Overdue and expired training raises a person's
[risk score](/employee-security/employees/risk-score); finishing lowers it,
more so on time. Every completed module earns
[points](/employee-security/employees/gamification), with a bonus when it's
on time.

## What employees see

Employees take courses from the link in their invitation, or in the employee
portal, where they sign in with the **Employee** role:

- **Home:** their pending trainings, and recognition: points, rank and badges.
- **My trainings:** **Pending** and **Completed**, with **Start**, **Resume**
  or **Retake**.
- **Training catalog:** approved courses they can take on their own.

At the end of a course they see whether they passed and can rate it. Ratings
appear in the library.
