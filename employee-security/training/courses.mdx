## The library

**Training › Library** lists every course by category: AI learning, AI
security, phishing, fraud prevention, security essentials, and more. Each
course shows who it's **Written for**, its length, and how employees rated it.
Courses come from three places:

| Origin | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog** | Built and kept up to date by NeuralTrust |
| **Custom** | Written or adapted by your team |
| **AI-generated** | Drafted with AI from your description and sources |

Click a course to **Preview** it as an employee would. Nothing you do in
preview is recorded.

## Languages

Courses can be in English, Spanish, German or French. Each employee gets a
course in their own language when it exists, then in your organization's
default, then in English. An employee's language comes from
[SCIM](/employee-security/connect/directory#scim); everyone else defaults to
English.

## How a course works

A course is a sequence of steps:

| Step | What the employee does |
| --- | --- |
| **Reading** | Reads a short explanation |
| **Video** | Watches a clip |
| **Quiz** | Answers questions |
| **Spot the signs** | Finds the red flags in a realistic message |
| **Decision** | Chooses what to do in a scenario |

To pass, the employee needs the course's **Pass mark** (80% by default).
Falling short restarts the course. A passed course stays valid for the months
set in **Settings › Employee Security › General** (12 by default); after that,
it counts as expired.

## Your own courses

With the **Editor** role you can:

- **Duplicate and edit** a catalog course to adapt it. Catalog and approved
  courses can't be changed in place: duplicate them to make a new version.
- Write a course from scratch: title, summary, category, audience, language,
  pass mark, and its steps.
- Record a **Presenter** narration with **Record now**.

Like simulation templates, new and changed courses need approval: **Submit for
review**, then an Employee Security **Admin** clicks **Approve** or **Reject**.
Only approved courses can be assigned.

## Draft a course with AI

**Training › Library › New training** drafts a course in four steps:

1. **Describe:** what it should teach, for whom, and how long. Add sources:
   files (PDF, DOCX, PPTX or TXT, up to 50 MB), links, **Web search**, or
   **Company personalization (OSINT)** from public information about your
   company. **Only use my sources** keeps it to what you provide.
2. **Outline:** review and adjust the structure.
3. **Style:** the visual style and the presenter: **Without presenter**, an
   **AI presenter**, or an **AI persona**.
4. **Review:** the draft is saved for review and approval like any other
   course.

## Collections

**Training › Collections** groups courses into a curriculum you can assign in
one go, such as an onboarding path. NeuralTrust's collections are read-only:
**Duplicate** one to change its modules.

## Compliance evidence

Tag a course with the frameworks it covers: GDPR, ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIS2,
DORA, and others. With [Vanta or Drata](/employee-security/connect/integrations#compliance-evidence)
connected, completions are sent as training evidence: Vanta gets each person's
status, and Drata a one-page completion certificate per course.
