## How it reaches your employees

TrustForce works through the mail platform you already have.

- **No mail routing changes.** With [Microsoft 365](/employee-security/connect/microsoft-365)
  or [Google Workspace](/employee-security/connect/google-workspace) connected,
  simulations and training invitations are placed straight into each mailbox
  through the provider's API. There is no MX change and no allow-listing in
  your email gateway.
- **The same connection scans the inbox.** Once a mail platform is connected,
  [email security](/employee-security/email-security/overview) watches every
  employee's mailbox.
- **Employees report with the buttons they know.** Gmail's **Report phishing**
  and Outlook's built-in **Report** button send
  [reports](/employee-security/report-suspicious-email) to TrustForce. There is
  no add-in to install.
- **People come from your directory.** Employees and groups sync from your
  NeuralTrust users, [SCIM, or your HR system](/employee-security/connect/directory).

## How the parts work together

Everything an employee does feeds one profile:

| Signal | Where it comes from | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clicked a simulation, or entered details | Phishing | Risk goes up; the lessons you chose are assigned |
| Reported a simulation | Phishing | Risk goes down; points on the leaderboard |
| Reported a real threat that you confirm | Email security | Risk goes down; points on the leaderboard |
| Finished training on time, or late | Training | Risk goes down, or less so |
| Targeted by real attacks | Email security | Exposure goes up |

The [risk score](/employee-security/employees/risk-score) combines all of this
with each person's role and access, so you can see who is most likely to be
compromised and how much damage that would cause.

## Who can do what

TrustForce has three roles, set in the platform's user management. Employees
themselves only see their own trainings and recognition, in the employee
portal. See [Roles and settings](/employee-security/administration/roles-and-settings).
