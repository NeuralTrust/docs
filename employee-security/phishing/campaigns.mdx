<p className="nt-lead">
  A campaign decides who gets which simulation and when. It can run once for
  everyone, or keep running and reach each new hire after they join.
</p>

**Phishing › Campaigns** lists every campaign with its status, audience size,
and how many people were sent, compromised (clicked) and reported. Click **New
campaign** to create one. You need the **Editor** role for Employee Security.

## Create a campaign

<Steps>

<Step title="Details">
Name the campaign and choose **How does it run?**

- **One-time:** sends once to everyone in the audience. It ends after the
  campaign duration set in **Settings › Employee Security › General** (14 days
  by default); anything still unsent then is cancelled.
- **Trigger-based:** keeps running and sends to people who join the audience
  later, such as new hires.
</Step>

<Step title="Audience">
**All employees**, or **Selected employees**: user groups, individuals, or
both. Only active employees in your directory can be targeted.
</Step>

<Step title="Content">
Pick up to 10 approved templates, all on the same channel. Each person gets
one of them, spread evenly across departments, and in the person's own
language when one of the templates is in it.

Email and QR campaigns can override the template's **Sending domain**, **From
name** and **From address**. Voice campaigns choose **Who calls**: each
template's persona, or one AI persona for all.
</Step>

<Step title="Schedule">
- **Start date** for a one-time campaign. A trigger-based one has **Start
  enrolling on** and **Days after joining** (0 to 365).
- **Spread sends over** none, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week (the default) or 2 weeks.
  Spreading keeps colleagues from comparing notes.
- **Send window:** **From** and **Until**, 09:00 to 17:00 by default, Monday
  to Friday, in the time zone shown.
- **Training:** **Assign a lesson to people who fail**: up to 5 lessons,
  assigned right after the failure, due in 7 days, with the usual reminders.
</Step>

<Step title="Review">
**Before it goes out** lists what still blocks the launch. Then click **Launch
now**, **Confirm & schedule** or **Activate**, depending on the schedule.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Launch checks

A campaign launches only when all of these hold:

- **Delivery connected** for its channel.
- **Audience inside your directory:** active employees only.
- **Every template uses the campaign channel.**
- **Every template approved.**
- For voice: voice simulations are turned on, and every persona that calls
  can speak.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Not launched yet |
| **Scheduled** | Launched, waiting for its start date |
| **Running** | Sending, or open for new people (trigger-based) |
| **Paused** | Sends stopped; **Resume** continues where it left off |
| **Completed** | Ended. Late clicks and reports still count |
| **Cancelled** | Stopped for good: **Cancel campaign** can't be undone |

## Test campaigns

From a campaign's **…** menu, **Mark as test** keeps its results out of
people's risk scores and the overview. **Count in metrics again** reverses it.
Use it for trial runs on your own team.

## Results

The campaign page shows, as people act:

| Metric | Out of |
| --- | --- |
| **Delivered** | People targeted |
| **Opened** | Messages delivered |
| **Clicked** | Messages delivered |
| **Submitted data** | Messages delivered |
| **Reported** | Messages delivered |

Results also break down by user group and by template. The **People** table
has each person's outcome:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Submitted data** | Typed into the landing page or pressed its button |
| **Clicked** | Opened the link, but submitted nothing |
| **Reported** | Reported the simulation |
| **Opened** | Opened it, did nothing else |
| **No action** | Delivered, no reaction |
| **Waiting to send** | Scheduled inside the spread or send window |
| **Not delivered** | The mail platform or phone network refused it |
| **Not sent** | Cancelled before sending, for example because the person was offboarded or the campaign ended |

See [What gets recorded](/employee-security/phishing/overview#what-gets-recorded)
for how each event is detected.
