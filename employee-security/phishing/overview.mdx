<p className="nt-lead">
  A simulation is a realistic attack that can't hurt anyone. It arrives like
  the real thing, records what the person does, and turns a mistake into a
  two-minute lesson instead of an incident.
</p>

## Channels

| Channel | How it arrives | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | In the inbox, placed through the mail platform's API | [Microsoft 365](/employee-security/connect/microsoft-365) or [Google Workspace](/employee-security/connect/google-workspace) |
| **QR code** | An email with a QR code to scan with a phone | Same as email |
| **Chat** | A Slack direct message from your Slack app | [Slack](/employee-security/connect/integrations#chat-and-notifications) |
| **SMS** | A text message to the person's phone | Turned on by NeuralTrust, on request |
| **Voice** | A phone call from an AI caller | Turned on by NeuralTrust, on request |

Email and QR simulations are not sent over SMTP: they are placed directly in
the mailbox, so they don't depend on your MX records, spam filters or gateway
allow-lists.

## What gets recorded

Each event counts once per person. A person's outcome is the riskiest thing
they did: someone who clicked and then reported shows as **Clicked**.

| Event | Recorded when |
| --- | --- |
| **Delivered** | The mail platform (or Slack, or the phone network) accepted the message |
| **Opened** | The email's images loaded. For a call, the person answered |
| **Clicked** | The person opened the link or scanned the QR code. For a call, they pressed a key when asked |
| **Submitted data** | The person typed into the landing page or pressed its button. For a call, they gave the caller what it asked for |
| **Reported** | The person reported the simulation with their mail app's Report button |

<Note>
**Opens are approximate.** Mail apps that block images never record an open,
and opens don't affect anyone's risk score. Clicks and submissions are what
count.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Security tools that open links count as clicks.** Placing the message in the
mailbox avoids URL rewriting by your email gateway, but a tool that follows
links after delivery, such as an API-based mail scanner or a link preview,
records a click for the person. Exclude your
[sending domains](/employee-security/phishing/sending-domains) from such
tools.
</Warning>

Simulation links stay valid for 30 days, so a click after the campaign ends
still counts.

## Nothing typed is stored

Landing pages look like the real thing but capture nothing: their fields aren't
sent anywhere. TrustForce records only that the person submitted the page.

## What the employee sees

<Steps>
<Step title="The landing page">
After clicking, the person lands on the simulated page: a sign-in portal, a
document viewer, a payment page, an MFA re-enrolment, and so on. It depends on
the template.
</Step>
<Step title="The failure page">
When they submit it, they learn it was a simulation: "No harm done". The page
shows the message they received, the red flags they missed, and tips for next
time, including how to report it with Outlook's or Gmail's Report button.
</Step>
<Step title="The lesson">
If the campaign assigns training to people who fail, the failure page links
the lesson. The person gets it in their language, due in 7 days, with the
usual reminders.
</Step>
</Steps>

People who report an email or QR simulation get a short email confirming it
was a simulation and that they did the right thing. It goes out on their first
report, and never for test campaigns.

## Results and risk

Campaign results feed each person's [risk score](/employee-security/employees/risk-score):
clicks and submissions raise it, more so for easy templates; reports lower it.
Results from campaigns marked as **Test** never count.

The **Phishing › Overview** page sums it up for the period you pick:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active simulations** | Campaigns scheduled, running or paused |
| **Sent** | Simulations sent in the period |
| **Compromise rate** | Share of messages sent where the person clicked the link. Entering details counts within it |
| **Report rate** | Share of messages sent that were reported |
