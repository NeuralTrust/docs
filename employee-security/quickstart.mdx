<p className="nt-lead">
  One pass from nothing to a first campaign with training attached. Start with
  a test campaign on a small group: its results don't count towards anyone's
  risk.
</p>

**Before you start** your team needs TrustForce in its plan. If **Employee
Security** is not in the console sidebar, it isn't included. To connect
integrations you need the **Admin** role for Employee Security; to run
campaigns, **Editor** is enough. Adding people and creating user groups also
need the platform's user management (IAM) **Admin** role. See
[Roles and settings](/employee-security/administration/roles-and-settings).

<Steps>

<Step title="Connect your mail platform">
Open **Settings › Employee Security › Integrations** and connect
[Microsoft 365](/employee-security/connect/microsoft-365) or
[Google Workspace](/employee-security/connect/google-workspace). This one
connection delivers simulations and training invitations, and turns on
[email security](/employee-security/email-security/overview) for every
employee's mailbox.
</Step>

<Step title="Bring your employees in">
Your NeuralTrust users already appear under **Employees › People**. To bring in
everyone else, connect [SCIM or your HR system](/employee-security/connect/directory),
or use **Add person** for a few people.

Create a user group with the people for your first campaign under
**Employees › User Groups › New user group**. Two or three colleagues who know
it's coming are enough.
</Step>

<Step title="Pick a simulation">
Open **Phishing › Templates**. Filter by channel **Email** and pick one in your
employees' language. Catalog templates are ready to use; anything you create
or change needs an admin's approval first. See
[Templates](/employee-security/phishing/templates).

Click **Send test** to see it in your own inbox.
</Step>

<Step title="Create the campaign">
Open **Phishing › Campaigns** and click **New campaign**.

1. **Details:** a name, and **One-time**.
2. **Audience:** **Selected employees**, then your test group.
3. **Content:** the template you picked.
4. **Schedule:** **Don't spread: everyone at once**. Under **Training**, turn on
   **Assign a lesson to people who fail** and pick a short lesson.
5. **Review:** check that everything under **Before it goes out** is ready.

Click **Launch now**. Then, from the campaign's **…** menu, choose **Mark as
test** so its results stay out of the risk scores and the overview.
</Step>

<Step title="Watch the results">
The campaign page fills in as people act: **Delivered**, **Opened**,
**Clicked**, **Submitted data** and **Reported**. Anyone who clicks lands on
the simulated page; anyone who submits it then sees a page that explains the
red flags and links the lesson. See
[Campaigns](/employee-security/phishing/campaigns#results).
</Step>

<Step title="Turn on reporting">
Employees report with their mail app's own buttons. Google Workspace needs
nothing; Microsoft 365 needs a reporting mailbox. See
[Report suspicious email](/employee-security/report-suspicious-email).
</Step>

</Steps>
