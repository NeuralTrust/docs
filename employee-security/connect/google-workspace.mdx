<p className="nt-lead">
  One entry in Google Admin. You authorize NeuralTrust's client ID for two
  Gmail scopes; there is no key to create or upload.
</p>

## What the connection does

- **Delivers simulations and training invitations** straight into each Gmail
  inbox through the Gmail API. There is no MX change and no allow-listing in
  Gmail or your email gateway.
- **Turns on [email security](/employee-security/email-security/overview).**
  Every employee's mailbox is watched for new mail, and suspicious messages
  can be quarantined, moved to Spam, or restored.
- **Picks up reports.** When an employee uses Gmail's **Report phishing** or
  **Report spam**, TrustForce sees it. See
  [Report suspicious email](/employee-security/report-suspicious-email#google-workspace).

TrustForce does not read your Google directory. Your employees come from
[your NeuralTrust users, SCIM or your HR system](/employee-security/connect/directory).

## Before you start

- A Google Workspace super admin, or an admin who can manage domain-wide
  delegation.
- Your email domains verified in NeuralTrust under
  [**Platform settings › SSO Configuration › Domains**](/platform/sso#part-3-verify-your-email-domain).
  TrustForce only reaches Gmail mailboxes on those domains.
- The **Admin** role for Employee Security in NeuralTrust.

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Copy the client ID and scopes">
Open **Settings › Employee Security › Integrations › Gmail**. The panel shows
NeuralTrust's **Client ID** and the two scopes to authorize:

| Scope | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.insert` | Placing simulations and training invitations in the inbox |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify` | Email security: watching mailboxes, reading new mail, labels, quarantine, and Gmail reports |

Both are required.
</Step>

<Step title="Authorize them in Google Admin">
Click **Open Google Admin**, or go to **Security › Access and data control ›
API controls › Domain-wide delegation** in the
[Google Admin console](https://admin.google.com). Click **Add new**, paste the
client ID and both scopes (comma-separated), and click **Authorize**.

Google can take a few minutes, sometimes longer, to apply a new delegation.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify the connection">
Back in NeuralTrust, enter an **Admin mailbox to test with**: any mailbox on a
verified domain. Click **Verify connection**.
</Step>

</Steps>

The status turns **Connected**, and email security starts watching mailboxes.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The test fails right after authorizing | Wait a few minutes for Google to apply the delegation, then try again |
| The test fails with a scope error | Both scopes were pasted exactly, with no spaces inside them |
| The test mailbox is rejected | It must be on a domain verified in NeuralTrust |
| Some employees never receive simulations | Their address is on a domain that isn't verified in NeuralTrust |
