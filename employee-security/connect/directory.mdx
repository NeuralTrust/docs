<p className="nt-lead">
  TrustForce needs to know who works for you, which team they're in and,
  ideally, their role and language. Pick the source that already holds that.
</p>

| Source | Best for | What it brings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NeuralTrust users](#neuraltrust-users) | Everyone who already signs in to NeuralTrust | Name, email, groups |
| [SCIM](#scim) | Your identity provider: Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, JumpCloud, OneLogin | Also department, title, phone, manager and language |
| [HR system](#hr-system) | Workday, BambooHR, Rippling, ADP, Namely, Gusto, Holded | Also start date, location and country, depending on the system |
| [Add person](#add-a-few-people-by-hand) | A few people | Email |

You can combine them. There is no CSV import.

## NeuralTrust users

Every member of your NeuralTrust organization, including pending invitations,
appears under **Employees › People**, with the groups they belong to. Groups
your identity provider syncs to NeuralTrust come along too. Someone removed
from the organization is offboarded in TrustForce.

People who should only take training and receive simulations can join with the
**Employee** role: it gives them the employee portal and nothing else. See
[Users & groups](/platform/users).

## SCIM

SCIM provisioning creates, updates and offboards employees from your identity
provider, and keeps groups in sync.

<Steps>

<Step title="Create a SCIM token">
Open **Settings › Employee Security › Integrations** and pick **SCIM
provisioning** under **Identity provisioning**. Copy the **SCIM base URL**,
then click **Create SCIM token**. The token is shown only once.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure your identity provider">
In your identity provider's SCIM app (Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, JumpCloud or
OneLogin), set the base URL and the token as the bearer token, assign the
users and groups to provision, and start provisioning.

Map `preferredLanguage` or `locale` if you have it: it decides the language of
each person's training and, where a template exists in it, of their
simulations.
</Step>

</Steps>

SCIM tokens are API keys with the **SCIM provisioning** scope. You'll find
them, and can revoke them, under **Settings › Employee Security › API keys**.

This SCIM endpoint feeds TrustForce only. To provision NeuralTrust console
users, see [SCIM provisioning](/platform/scim).

## HR system

An HR system sync brings the richest profiles: title, department, manager,
start date, location and country, as far as your system provides them.
Departments become user groups that update themselves.

1. Open **Settings › Employee Security › Integrations** and pick your HR
   system under **HRIS**. Only one HR system can be connected at a time.
2. Follow the panel's **Before you start** and **Permissions requested**, then
   click **Save and test**.

The first sync runs when you connect. After that it runs nightly, or right
away with **Sync now**.

<Note>
**A safety check protects you from a bad export.** If a sync returns fewer
than half of the active people it manages (once there are at least 5),
TrustForce changes nothing instead of offboarding everyone missing from it.
</Note>

Once you have verified email domains, the HR sync creates people only on
them. It offboards only the people it created.

## Add a few people by hand

**Employees › People › Add person** invites someone to NeuralTrust by email,
with the **Employee** role and, optionally, enrolls them in training or a
running simulation. It needs the platform's user management (IAM) **Admin**
role. Use it for a handful of people; for everyone else, connect a source.

## When someone leaves

Offboarding is never a hard delete. The person's status becomes
**Offboarded**: they stop receiving simulations and reminders, pending sends
are cancelled, and their mailbox is no longer scanned. Their
history stays, so past campaign and training results keep adding up.

## User groups

Groups target campaigns and training, and break down results:

- **Synced** groups come from your identity provider or HR system.
- Groups you build yourself, in **Employees › User Groups › New user group**,
  hold the people you pick. Like **Add person**, this needs the platform's IAM
  **Admin** role.

See [People and groups](/employee-security/employees/people).
