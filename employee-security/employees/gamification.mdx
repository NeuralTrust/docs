<p className="nt-lead">
  Reporting is the habit you want most, so it pays. Employees earn points and
  badges for reporting phishing and keeping up with training, and see where
  they stand on a leaderboard.
</p>

## Points

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Report a simulated phish | +50 |
| Report a real phish, once it's confirmed malicious | +100 |
| Complete a training module | +30 |
| Finish training before the due date | +15 on top |

**Employees › Gamification › How points are earned** always shows the current
values.

## Badges

| Badge | Earned for |
| --- | --- |
| **First catch** | Reporting a first phish |
| **Deepfake detective** | Reporting 3 AI voice simulations |
| **On time, every time** | No overdue training for a year |
| **Triage hero** | 5 real threats reported and confirmed |
| **Streak: 6 months** | No simulation failures for six months |
| **Team player** | Their department reaching 95% training completion |

## Leaderboard

**Employees › Gamification › Leaderboard** ranks **Individuals**, or **User
Groups** by their points per person, for the **Quarter**, the **Year** or
**All time**. Ties share a rank.

Employees see their own points, rank and badges on the home page of the
[employee portal](/employee-security/training/assignments#what-employees-see).

Results from campaigns marked as **Test** never earn points.
