## People

**Employees › People** lists everyone, with filters for **Status**, **Risk**
and **User Group**:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Risk score** | 0 to 100, lower is better. See [Risk score](/employee-security/employees/risk-score) |
| **Simulations** | Simulations received |
| **Compromises** | Share of delivered simulations they clicked |
| **Reports** | Share of delivered simulations they reported |
| **Last failure** | When they last failed a simulation |
| **Training** | Their training status |

**Offboarded** people have left: their history is kept, and nothing is sent
to them. Use the **Status** filter to see them.

Select people to act on them together: **Assign training**, **Assign
phishing** (to a running campaign), or **Add to user group**. Adding to a
group needs the platform's user management (IAM) **Admin** role.

## A person's profile

Click someone to open their profile:

- **Overview:** their risk score and what drives it, plus email, user groups,
  manager, phone, start date, location and country, depending on your
  directory source.
- **Training:** their courses and status. **Assign training**, **Remove
  training** or **Send reminder**.
- **Phishing:** the simulations they received and what they did. **Remove
  from simulation** takes them out of one that hasn't reached them yet.
- **Activity:** a timeline of everything that changed their risk: failures,
  reports, completed training, real threats they received.

## User groups

**Employees › User Groups** lists every group with its members, average risk
score, where it comes from, its simulation, report and training numbers, and
which campaigns and assignments use it.

| Type | Source |
| --- | --- |
| **Synced** | Your identity provider, through NeuralTrust users or SCIM, or departments from your HR system. They update themselves |
| **Picked by hand** | Created with **New user group** |

Groups are how you target campaigns and assignments, and how results break
down in every overview. They are the platform's user groups, so creating and
changing them needs the platform's user management (IAM) **Admin** role; see
[Users & groups](/platform/users).
