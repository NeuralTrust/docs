<p className="nt-lead">
  One number per person, from 0 to 100, lower is better. It answers two
  questions together: how likely is this person to fall for an attack, and how
  bad would it be if they did?
</p>

## Bands

| Band | Score |
| --- | --- |
| **Low** | Under 30 |
| **Medium** | 30 to 54 |
| **High** | 55 to 74 |
| **Critical** | 75 and above |

Someone with no history yet starts at the low end of **Medium**: no evidence
either way.

## What goes into it

The score combines **likelihood** and **impact**. A careless intern and a
careful CFO can end up with similar scores for opposite reasons, and the
profile shows which.

### Likelihood: behavior and exposure

**Behavior** is what the person does, and weighs most:

| Signal | Raises the score | Lowers it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Simulation results** | Clicking, and more so entering details. Failing an easy simulation weighs more than failing a hard one. Two or more failures in 90 days add a penalty | Reporting simulations. Reporting one after failing it softens the failure |
| **Threat response** | Letting real threats and simulations go unreported | Reporting them, and real threats count double once your team confirms them |
| **Awareness training** | Overdue, expired, or never started training | Finishing training on time |

**Exposure** is how much attackers aim at the person: how many real threats
reach them, whether they're targeted by business email compromise or
impersonated, whether they're among the most targeted people in your
organization, and how visible their mailbox is, for example through shared
inboxes.

### Impact: role and access

How much damage a compromised account could do: the person's role, and the
access their groups give them, from mailing lists to privileged groups. Roles
and group tiers are estimated with AI from job titles, group names, number of
direct reports and tenure, never from names or email addresses.

## How it changes over time

- It updates as things happen: a click, a report, a completed course.
- Recent behavior counts most. Simulation results lose weight after 3 months
  and stop counting after a year; training counts over the last 12 months; real
  threats over the last 90 days.
- It's recalculated nightly, so ageing results fade without anyone acting.

## Where you see it

- **Employees › People:** each person's score, with a **Risk** filter.
- **A person's profile:** the score and its branches, **Exposure** and
  **Resilience**, so you can see what drives it.
- **Employees › User Groups:** the average of each group's active members.
- **Phishing › Overview:** the human risk score over time, risk by user group,
  and the highest-risk employees.

Results from campaigns marked as **Test** never affect it.
