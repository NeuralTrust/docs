<p className="nt-lead">
  Your mail filter stops most attacks. Email security is for the ones that get
  through: it reads what reaches each inbox, flags what looks wrong, and lets
  your team pull every copy out with one decision.
</p>

It's included, and on as soon as
[Microsoft 365](/employee-security/connect/microsoft-365) or
[Google Workspace](/employee-security/connect/google-workspace) is connected.
There's nothing to route: TrustForce reads mailboxes through the same
connection, after delivery.

## What it watches

Every active employee's mailbox. New mail is picked up as it arrives, through
the provider's change notifications, with a periodic catch-up so nothing is
missed if a notification is lost. Sent mail is read only to learn who your
employees write to, so a known contact can be told from a first-time sender.

If a mailbox can't be watched, **Email security › Settings › Inbox scanning**
says so, and **View mailboxes** lists each one with its status: **Active**,
**Failed** or **Expired**.

Messages that can't be fully read, such as those over 50 MB, are flagged as
**Not fully scanned** instead of being treated as safe.

## What it checks

- **Sender authentication:** SPF, DKIM, DMARC and ARC.
- **Impersonation:** look-alike domains, display names that copy a colleague
  or an executive, and senders you've never heard from.
- **Links and attachments:** known-bad links and domains, macros, suspicious
  PDFs and archives.
- **Detection rules:** a large, maintained rule set for phishing, malware,
  business email compromise and spam.
- **Reputation:** file hashes and domains are looked up in VirusTotal. Nothing
  is uploaded.
- **AI reading:** for mail that rules alone can't settle, an AI model reads a
  redacted excerpt and suggests a verdict. The excerpt is not stored.

Mail that looks fine leaves only counters, sender history and a short-lived
index (see [Data handling](/employee-security/administration/data-handling)).
Mail that looks wrong becomes a **detection** in **Email security › Emails**.

## What happens to flagged mail

By default, nothing moves on the scanner's say-so. Flagged mail waiting for a
decision gets a **Suspicious** category in Outlook or label in Gmail, so the
person sees a warning in their own mail app (turn it off in **Settings ›
Triage › Tag flagged emails as Suspicious**). The message itself is never
modified.

A decision moves it, and covers every copy of the email in every mailbox, plus
copies that arrive in the next 30 days:

| Decision | Effect on every copy |
| --- | --- |
| **Malicious** | Quarantined |
| **Spam** | Moved to Junk (Outlook) or Spam (Gmail) |
| **Safe** | Left in the inbox, or restored if quarantined |

Quarantine is a hidden folder (Outlook) or a hidden label (Gmail) in the same
mailbox. In Gmail the message leaves the inbox but can still turn up in an
**All Mail** search. Nothing is deleted, and **Restore to inbox** puts it
back. The employee isn't notified.

Decisions come from your team in [triage](/employee-security/email-security/triage),
from auto-triage, or from the reporting threshold.

## Auto-triage

**Email security › Settings › Triage › Auto-triage with AI** decides for you
when the AI is confident:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | Your team decides everything (the default). Reports still show the AI assessment to help |
| **Shadow** | Reads and measures, never acts. Compare its calls with your team's under **Agreement** |
| **Active** | Applies its verdicts: **Malicious: quarantine everywhere** (on by default) and **Spam: move to junk** (off by default). **Also apply to what is already waiting** clears the backlog |

Start in **Shadow** for a couple of weeks, check the agreement, then switch to
**Active**.

## Reported by many

When several different employees report the same email within 7 days (3 by
default), it's escalated to the top of triage. With auto-triage **Active**, it's
quarantined right away. Set the threshold to 0 to turn it off.

## Block and allow senders

**Email security › Senders** holds your own rules: **Block** an address or
domain to always treat its mail as malicious, or **Allow** one to trust it.

## Alerts

With [Slack or Microsoft Teams](/employee-security/connect/integrations#chat-and-notifications)
connected, each malicious email sends one alert to your channel: detected,
reported and confirmed, or a quarantine that failed.

## The overview

**Email security › Overview** shows emails scanned, what needs triage, what's
quarantined, what couldn't be fully scanned, time to triage, and reported
simulations, plus threat types, the most targeted employees, and recent
threats.
