<p className="nt-lead">
  One queue for everything suspicious: emails your employees reported and
  emails the scanner flagged. Each decision applies to every copy, and the
  people who reported it hear how it turned out.
</p>

Open **Email security › Emails**. You need the **Editor** role for Employee
Security to decide.

## The list

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Triage** | Waiting for a decision |
| **Malicious** / **Spam** | Decided |
| **Simulation** | Reports of your own phishing simulations |
| **All** | Everything |

Each row shows the sender, subject, verdict, whether copies are
**Quarantined** or **Delivered**, how many reports it has, and who reported it.
Scanner finds show **Automatic** as the reporter. An **Escalated** badge means
enough different people reported it to cross the
[reported-by-many threshold](/employee-security/email-security/overview#reported-by-many).

### Verdicts

Before a decision, the verdict is a reading: **Likely malicious**,
**Suspicious**, **Likely spam** or **Likely safe**. After it, it's what you
decided: **Malicious**, **Spam** or **Benign**. Reports of your own
simulations show **Simulation** and need nothing from you.

## Read the evidence

Click a row to open it. The panel has three tabs:

- **Summary:** the **AI assessment** ("Suggested by AI. You decide.") and the
  automated analysis behind it.
- **Evidence:** sender authentication, links, and **Reputation** lookups.
- **Email:** who sent it and who reported it, when, **Sender history**
  (whether this sender has written to your organization before), and the
  start of its text.

## Decide

| Decision | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirm malicious** | Quarantines every copy. Credits the people who reported it: their risk score goes down and they earn points |
| **Mark as spam** | Moves every copy to Junk or Spam |
| **Mark as safe** | Leaves or restores every copy in the inbox |
| **Resolve with no action** | Closes it without touching any mailbox |

**Apply and go to next** keeps you moving through the queue. **Actions**
applies **Malicious**, **Spam** or **Benign** to several rows at once.

The people who reported the email get an email with the outcome (malicious,
safe or spam), in English or Spanish. **Resolve with no action** sends
nothing.

For a scanner detection you can also act on copies directly: **Quarantine**,
**Quarantine in all N mailboxes**, **Restore to inbox**, or **Safe: restore in
all mailboxes**.

## View the original

On a scanner detection, **View original** reads the full message live from
the mailbox, for when the stored evidence isn't enough. It's for Employee
Security **Admins** only, asks for a reason, and is recorded in the
[platform logs](/platform/audit-logs). Nothing extra is stored. It's not
available once retention has cleared the detection's details.

## Add a reported email by hand

For a report that arrived another way, such as forwarded to your security
team, click **Add reported email**:

1. Upload the message as an **.eml** file, up to 10 MB. In Outlook on the web
   or the new Outlook, download it from the email's **…** menu; in Gmail, use
   **More › Download message**. Classic Outlook saves a **.msg**, which isn't
   accepted.
2. Enter who **Reported by**.
3. Click **Analyze and add**.

If the email is one of your simulations, TrustForce says so and counts it as a
good report for that person.

Employees don't need this: they report with their mail app's own buttons. See
[Report suspicious email](/employee-security/report-suspicious-email).
