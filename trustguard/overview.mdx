  <div className="nt-section">
    <h2 className="nt-section-title">FAQ</h2>
    <details className="nt-faq">
      <summary>Is TrustGuard an AI gateway?</summary>
      <p>
        No. It evaluates traffic and returns a verdict. The collector applies it. When
        that collector is TrustGate, the gateway also routes the call.
      </p>
    </details>
    <details className="nt-faq">
      <summary>Do I need TrustGate?</summary>
      <p>
        No. Use another supported gateway, an SDK, middleware, or a coding-agent plugin.
        See <a href="/integrations/overview">Integrations</a>.
      </p>
    </details>
    <details className="nt-faq">
      <summary>Can I evaluate a policy without blocking traffic?</summary>
      <p>
        Yes. Observe records findings and downgrades block, ask, and transform to
        report. Switch to Enforce after Activity looks right.
      </p>
    </details>
    <details className="nt-faq">
      <summary>Can TrustGuard inspect tool calls?</summary>

        in <a href="/trustguard/how-it-works">How it works</a>.
