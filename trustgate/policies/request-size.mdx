---
title: "Request size"
description: "Reject oversized requests before they go anywhere — a cheap guard against accidental and abusive payloads."
---

Applies to **LLM and MCP**.

A request whose body is over the limit is refused on the way in, before any
provider is contacted. It catches a client bug that stuffed a document into a
prompt, and a caller doing it on purpose.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Max payload size** | 10 MB | The body, in bytes, kilobytes or megabytes. |
| **Max characters** | 100,000 | Characters in the request body, prompt and message history included. |
| **Require Content-Length** | off | Refuse requests that do not declare their size. |

Run it gateway-wide as a ceiling, and target a tighter one at applications whose
traffic should be small. It is not a substitute for model limits: prompts that do
not fit the resolved model are already rejected on the chat path.
