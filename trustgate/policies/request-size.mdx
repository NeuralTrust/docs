---
title: "Request size"
description: "Reject oversized requests before they go anywhere — a cheap guard against accidental and abusive payloads."
---

Applies to **LLM and MCP**.

A request whose body is over the limit is refused before any policy runs and
before any provider is contacted. It costs nothing to evaluate and catches two
things: a client bug that stuffed a document into a prompt, and a caller trying
to.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Max payload size** | 10 MB | The body, in bytes, kilobytes or megabytes. |
| **Max characters** | 100,000 | Characters in the request content. |
| **Require Content-Length** | off | Refuse requests that do not declare their size. |

Run it gateway-wide as a ceiling, and target a tighter one at applications whose
traffic should be small. A context-window preflight on the chat path already
rejects prompts that cannot fit the resolved model, so this is not about model
limits — it is about not reading the body at all.
